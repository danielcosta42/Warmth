# Warmth: Campfires & Camps for WoW: Forever

Somewhere out there, someone just lit a campfire. Warmth puts it on your map: what the camp offers, who is sitting around it, and the way there. Light your own, call people over, and watch the circle fill up.

## What it does

- **Campfires on your map.** A glowing pin for every campfire a player chose to announce, on the world map and the minimap. The more people around the fire, the brighter it glows.
- **See the camp before you walk.** The campfire's tier, which camp objects are there and what they give (rested experience from a Camp Tent, repairs from a Repair Bot, stat buffs...), how many places are still open, and who is in the circle.
- **Go there.** One click sets the game's waypoint on the fire.
- **Find what you need.** Filter the list: camps with a tent, with repairs, or with an open place for your object.
- **Set up your own camp.** A checklist of what is in your bags: campfire kits and their wood, the camp objects your professions made, and what you can cook right now. Then announce it, with a word for whoever is reading.
- **The circle fills up.** People who walk up to your fire appear around it, with the object they brought. An empty seat marked "you" waits on every camp you look at.
- **Zone copies.** Forever runs several copies of a zone at once. Warmth marks camps that are in another copy, and lets you ask the owner for a group invite to land in theirs.
- **A heads-up nearby.** A quiet toast when someone announces a campfire in the zone you are in.
- **The Workshop.** Every camp object your professions can make: ready to craft, missing materials, missing skill or recipe, and what each one takes. For what you lack it says the easiest way to get it: the nearest vendor of your faction, the zones with the most nodes, the creature that drops it, or what it is made of. One click opens the profession, marks the vendor or opens the zone's map.
- **Ask, answer, thank.** A camp tells the network which buff objects it still lacks, and players nearby who carry one or can craft it get a heads-up. Sit at someone's fire and you can thank the host; hosts gather warmth and rise from Kindling to Beacon. A guestbook keeps the camps you sat at.
- **Benefits that count for you.** The camp view marks a benefit you would not gain because you already carry the class buff it does not stack with.
- **A compass and a ring of camp actions.** A small campfire on your screen points to the most useful fire nearby. It counts down your minute of rest and then shows how long your benefits last. Click it and a ring opens: light your kit, place your camp object, unroll the sleeping bag, sit, cook.

## You decide what is shared

- When you light a campfire, Warmth tells the network, so others can find it. One switch in the options turns that off; then a campfire is announced only when you click.
- Warmth asks **once** whether you want to appear in other people's circles when you sit at an announced fire. Say no and you never show up in anyone's circle; change your mind in the options.
- A vendor of camp materials you open that Warmth does not know yet is told to the network later, never on the spot; one switch turns that off.
- You can turn off invite requests, the toast, the pins and most of the motion.

## Getting in

Warmth lives in the game's **addon compartment** (the addon list by the minimap): click to open, right click to set up a camp. Or type `/warmth`.

- `/warmth setup`: the camp checklist
- `/warmth options`: the options
- `/warmth demo`: three demo camps on your map that only you see, to look around (`/warmth clear` removes them)

## Good to know

- Warmth runs on **ChehulNet**, the network shared with GuildOS, Lodestar and PartyLens. It is brand new on this network: for now you will mostly hear campfires from your guild, your group and a few players beyond. The reach grows as the other addons update.
- Like every ChehulNet addon, Warmth says hello to the network from time to time (your class, level and zone), which is how its addons find each other. Your campfire and your place in a circle are never part of that.
- Only campfires lit by players show up, and only the announced ones. Campfires that belong to the world are not listed.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese. No dependencies.

## Em português

Em algum lugar, alguém acabou de acender uma fogueira. O Warmth põe ela no seu mapa: o que o acampamento oferece, quem está sentado em volta e o caminho até lá. Acenda a sua, chame gente e veja a roda encher.

- **Fogueiras no mapa**: um pino brilhante para cada fogueira que um jogador escolheu anunciar, no mapa e no minimapa. Quanto mais gente em volta, mais forte o brilho.
- **Veja o acampamento antes de ir**: o nível da fogueira, os objetos de acampamento e o que eles dão (experiência de descanso da tenda, conserto do Repair Bot, bônus de atributos...), quantos lugares estão livres e quem está na roda.
- **Ir até lá**: um clique põe o marcador do jogo na fogueira.
- **Filtros**: com tenda, com conserto ou com lugar livre para o seu objeto.
- **Monte o seu acampamento**: um checklist do que há na bolsa (kits e madeira, os objetos das suas profissões, o que dá para cozinhar agora) e o anúncio, com um recado.
- **A roda enche**: quem chega ao seu fogo aparece em volta dele, com o objeto que trouxe.
- **Cópias da zona**: o Warmth marca os acampamentos que estão em outra cópia e deixa você pedir ao dono um convite de grupo para cair na dele.
- **Aviso de proximidade** quando anunciam uma fogueira na sua zona.
- **Bússola e roda de ações**: uma fogueirinha na tela aponta para a fogueira mais útil por perto; um clique abre a roda para acender o kit, pôr o seu objeto, usar o saco de dormir, sentar e cozinhar.

**Você decide o que é compartilhado.** Ao acender uma fogueira o Warmth avisa a rede, para os outros acharem; uma opção desliga isso, e aí ela só é anunciada com o seu clique. O Warmth pergunta uma vez se você quer aparecer nas rodas dos outros; se disser não, você nunca aparece na roda de ninguém.

Abra pelo **compartimento de addons** do jogo (clique: abrir; clique direito: montar acampamento) ou com `/warmth`. Roda na **ChehulNet**, a mesma rede do GuildOS, do Lodestar e do PartyLens; como é novo nela, por enquanto você ouve mais a sua guilda, o seu grupo e alguns outros.
